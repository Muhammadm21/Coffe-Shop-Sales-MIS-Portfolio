This project transforms raw transactional data into an actionable executive reporting tool. By bypassing manual macros and leveraging a strictly modern Business Intelligence workflow (Power Query, the Excel Data Model, and DAX), the dashboard provides automated, scalable insights into operational bottlenecks, peak traffic periods, and product profitability.

## Technical Workflow

⚬	Data Engineering (Power Query): Extracted and transformed a flat file of raw transactional data. Engineered core metrics like Total Sales, and extracted granular time intelligence (Hour, Day of Week, Month Name) to enable deep time-series analysis.

⚬	Data Modeling & DAX: Loaded the transformed data directly into the Excel Data Model. Authored custom DAX measures to calculate dynamic, scalable KPIs including Total Revenue, Total Orders, Items Sold, and Average Ticket Size.

⚬	Interactive Reporting: Implemented global slicers linked via Report Connections, allowing executives to instantly cross-filter the entire dataset by specific Store Locations and Product Categories.

## Core Business Insights

⚬	Peak Hourly Traffic: Mapped order volume across a 24-hour clock to pinpoint the exact timing of the morning rush, enabling data-driven barista staffing optimization.

⚬	Revenue vs. Volume: Constructed a dual-axis combo chart to analyze the correlation between total items sold and gross revenue over a 6-month timeline.

⚬	Top 10 Best-Selling Products: Isolated the highest-grossing specific menu items, providing granular data to guide inventory purchasing and supply chain preparation.

⚬	Average Ticket Size by Category: Analyzed customer spending habits per category to identify the most lucrative upselling opportunities.
